T H E C R E A T I V E E N G I N E
The 5 Creative Director brains
For every brief, the system doesn't just write one caption — it generates five,
each from a different Saudi creative philosophy. Then it scores them and picks
the best based on what's worked before for that sector and occasion.
C D - 0 1
Firaasa Architect
HOW IT THINKS
Starts from a behavioral
observation — it spots
something true about the
customer and says it back to
them. Begins with "ﻲﻠﻟا" (the
one who) or a present-tense
verb. No brand name until
the end.
EXAMPLE
ء —ﺎﺗﺷﻟا ﺎﻓد ﻰﻠﻋ دورﯾ ﻲﻠﻟا
ل.ﺑﻣرﻛ ﻲﻓ هﺎﻘﻠﯾ
C D - 0 2
Metaphor Architect
HOW IT THINKS
Opens with "إذا" or "ﻲﻌﻣ لﯾﺧﺗ"
— builds a metaphor that
reframes the product as
something bigger. Poetic,
not literal. Works best when
the occasion has emotional
weight (Eid, National Day).
EXAMPLE

Authenticity
Detective
HOW IT THINKS
Starts with "ﺎّﻣﻟ" (with shadda

Heritage Decoder
HOW IT THINKS
Connects the brand to Saudi

Paradox Hunter
HOW IT THINKS
Subverts an expectation in

second. Creates a small
surprise that stops scrolling.
Best for sales events,
competitive positioning,
evergreen content.
EXAMPLE

The router
After generating all 5, the router picks the
best based on past performance. It knows
that F&B Ramadan responds best to
Authenticity (CD-03) and Firaasa (CD-01),
while Fashion White Friday loves Paradox
(CD-05). These scores improve every time a
human approves or rejects an output.
The quality gate
Every caption passes 10 automatic checks
before it's accepted: Saudi dialect purity,
correct length, emoji count, brand mention
present, forbidden words absent, no English
words, no generic greetings, opener
uniqueness, and more. Score is 0–100. Only
high-scorers get saved.
